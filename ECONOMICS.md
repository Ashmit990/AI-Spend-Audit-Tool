# Economic Model & Unit Economics

## Lead Value to Credex
Credex earns revenue by taking a 10–15% margin on the AI credits it sells in bulk.
- **Average Startup AI Spend:** $1,500/mo ($18,000/yr).
- **Credex Discount to Founder:** 20%.
- **Credex Net Revenue per Startup:** ~5% of spend ($900/yr).
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Estimated 3 years → **$2,700 LTV**.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Since the tool is a lead-generation magnet, the "CAC" is essentially the cost of developing and maintaining the tool plus any marketing spend.
- **Organic Social (Twitter/HN):** $0 direct spend.
- **LinkedIn Targeted Ads (Future):** Target $15 per qualified audit completion.
- **Convert Rate from Audit to Lead:** 15%.
- **Cost per Lead (CPL):** ~$100.
- **Lead to Closed Deal Conversion:** 10%.
- **Blended CAC:** ~$1,000.
- **LTV/CAC Ratio:** 2.7x (Healthy for a seed-stage startup).

## Conversion Funnel Math
1.  **Visitors:** 10,000 / mo
2.  **Audit Completions (40%):** 4,000 / mo
3.  **High-Intent Leads (15%):** 600 / mo (Emails captured with >$500/mo savings)
4.  **Consultations Booked (5% of leads):** 30 / mo
5.  **Closed Deals (30% of calls):** 9 new customers / mo

## Path to $1M ARR (18 Months)
To reach $1M ARR, Credex needs approximately 1,111 active startup customers (at $900 net revenue/yr).

**Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Foundation**
- Focus: Improve Audit → Lead conversion from 15% to 25%.
- Target: 200 customers.
- Primary Channel: Hacker News, Product Hunt, and VC Portfolio Intros.

**Phase 2 (Months 7-12): Scaling**
- Focus: Diversify "Tools Audited" to include GPU clouds (AWS, Bedrock, GCP).
- Target: 600 customers.
- Primary Channel: SEO and Paid Search.

**Phase 3 (Months 13-18): Dominance**
- Focus: Automated monthly audits (B2B SaaS product).
- Target: 1,100+ customers.
- Primary Channel: Referral loop ("Invite another founder to audit their spend").

## Critical Success Factors
For this math to hold true, the following must be true:
- **Retention:** Logo churn must stay below 2% per month.
- **Trust:** The audit engine must remain "neutral." If founders feel it's just a biased sales pitch for Credex, the completion rate will plummet.
- **API Market Stability:** Providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) must continue to allow third-party bulk credit provisioning.
