# Product Metrics & Instrumentation

## North Star Metric: "Total Identified Annual Savings"
The single most important number for this tool is the sum of annual savings identifying for all users.
- **Why?** This metric directly measures the *value* the tool provides to the market. If this number is low, the tool is either not reaching the right high-spend users or the audit logic isn't aggressive enough. High savings numbers lead to better social sharing and higher lead quality for Credex.

## 3 Input Metrics
1.  **Audit Completion Rate (ACR):** The percentage of users who land on the page and successfully reach the result screen. 
    - *Goal:* >35%. Low ACR indicates the form is too complex or the "Aha!" moment is too far away.
2.  **Lead Conversion Rate (LCR):** The percentage of audit completers who provide their email at the end.
    - *Goal:* >12%. This measures the trust we've built and the relevance of the "Credex" value proposition.
3.  **Viral Coefficient (K-factor):** The average number of new users generated by a single user sharing their report link.
    - *Goal:* >0.2. While not a "viral app," a small boost from LinkedIn/X shares significantly lowers our effective CAC.

## What to Instrument First
- **PostHog / Mixpanel Events:**
  - `form_step_completed`: To identify which field (e.g., "Add Tool" or "Team Size") causes people to drop off.
  - `audit_calculated`: Triggered when the logic runs successfully.
  - `cta_clicked`: Tracking clicks on the "Talk to Credex" or "Share Link" buttons.
  - `error_triggered`: To track API failures with Anthropic or Resend.

## The Pivot Trigger
**The "Pivot" Number:** If the **Median Annual Savings** found per audit falls below **$200**, we should pivot the tool.
- **Reasoning:** If most startups are already optimized or the pricing across the industry settles into a "perfect" $20/mo flat fee with no consolidation opportunities, the tool loses its utility. In that case, we would pivot the engine to focus on **Usage Efficiency** (e.g., "How many tokens are your developers wasting on low-quality prompts?") rather than simple **Subscription Spend**.
