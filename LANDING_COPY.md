## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1.  **Do I need to connect my credit card or bank?**
    No. This tool is purely input-based. You simply tell us what you're using, and we run the math based on current market pricing. No private financial access required.
2.  **Is my data private?**
    Your audit results are stored anonymously by default. If you choose to share your report via the unique link, your email and company name are stripped from the public version.
3.  **How do you calculate the savings?**
    We use a deterministic engine that cross-references your team size and usage against the latest publicly available pricing for 8+ major AI tools (updated May 2026).
4.  **What is Credex?**
    Credex is a partner company that helps startups access enterprise-grade AI credits at a discount. We use this tool to show you where Credex can help you save even more.
5.  **How often is the pricing updated?**
    Our database is manually verified and updated whenever major providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) change their pricing structures. Last update: May 24, 2026.
