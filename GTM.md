# Go-To-Market Strategy (GTM)

## Target User Profile
The primary user for the AI Spend Audit tool is the **Technical Founder or CTO of a Seed to Series B startup (5–50 employees).** 

- **The Pain Point:** Their "SaaS sprawl" is getting out of control. They’ve gone from 1 AI tool to 10 in the last 18 months. They suspect they are double-paying for various LLM seats but don't have the time to manually audit individual receipts.
- **The Psychology:** They are "cost-conscious but speed-addicted." They won't spend 4 hours on a spreadsheet to save $200, but they *will* spend 45 seconds on a web app to see if they're being "ripped off."

## Search Intent & SEO
Before landing on our tool, these users are Googling:
- "Cursor vs Windsurf pricing for teams"
- "Claude 3.5 Sonnet API vs ChatGPT Pro subscription cost"
- "How to cut startup AI spend"
- "Is GitHub Copilot Enterprise worth it for 10 people?"
- "Anthropic Claude Team plan vs Pro"

Our tool serves as the definitive answer to these comparative queries by providing a personalized consolidation report.

## Where They Hang Out
1.  **Hacker News:** High concentration of CTOs and developers who appreciate "math-first" tools without marketing fluff.
2.  **X/Twitter (Tech Twitter):** Founders following AI influencers (e.g., Greg Brockman, Andrej Karpathy) often share efficiency tips.
3.  **Specific Disord/Slack Communities:** Y Combinator (Bookface), Founders Network, and AI/ML-focused Discords like LangChain or Pinecone.
4.  **Subreddits:** r/startups, r/SaaS, and r/ChatGPTCoding.

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)
- **Day 1-3 (Build):** Launch the "Beta" to 5 local founder friends for feedback.
- **Day 5 (The Launch):** Post on Hacker News with a "Show HN" title: "Show HN: I built a tool to see if you're overpaying for AI subs." Respond to every comment.
- **Day 10 (Direct Outreach):** Search Twitter for people complaining about "OpenAI bill" or "Cursor pricing" and reply with a link to their audit.
- **Day 15 (Partnership):** DM founders of complementary "Free for Startups" directories (like FounderPass) to get included in their cost-optimization lists.
- **Day 20 (The Viral Hook):** Encourage shares on LinkedIn by highlighting "We saved $X,000" in the OG image.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
Our **Unfair Advantage** is the relationship with **Credex**. Because Credex already sells discounted credits, we can position the audit as a "neutral diagnostic" that leads directly into their "pharmacy" (the solution). By partnering with **Venture Capital Portfolios**, we can offer this tool as a "perk" to their entire portfolio of 200+ companies. VCs love cost-cutting tools because it extends the runway of their investments.
